# YouTube SEO and Discovery
1. Translate the idea into a clear viewer question and primary search intent.
2. Compare YouTube autocomplete, public search results and competitor packaging; record dates and sources.
3. Draft accurate titles with the promise and differentiator early; avoid false urgency or claims.
4. Put a truthful summary in the first description lines; add chapters, links and disclosures only when accurate.
5. Use relevant tags where useful; do not keyword-stuff or promise tags drive ranking.
6. Align thumbnail imagery/text with the actual video and prepare controlled alternatives.
7. Check mobile readability, accessibility, language, spelling and policy risk.

Optimize for satisfied viewers and accurate expectations, not CTR alone.
