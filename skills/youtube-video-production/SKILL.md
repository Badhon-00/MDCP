# YouTube Video Production
Create a brief with format, aspect ratio, duration, hook, script, scene list, narration, B-roll, captions, sound design and thumbnail direction.

- Keep source media read-only; work in a separate project/output directory.
- Use FFmpeg for deterministic trim, concat, resize, loudness normalization, subtitles and render operations; use a professional NLE for complex timeline edits.
- Shorts: validate 9:16 framing, safe margins, readable captions and immediate context.
- Long-form: validate 16:9 framing, pacing, chapters, audio consistency and end-screen space.
- Export a review copy. Check decode errors, duration, resolution, audio, caption timing and thumbnail dimensions.

Never use unlicensed music, footage, fonts or images. Preserve asset attribution and licenses.
