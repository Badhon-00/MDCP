# YouTube Policy and Safety Review
Before enabling a workflow, check current YouTube Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, API Services Terms, developer policies, copyright rules and applicable privacy requirements.

Review rights to video/audio/images/fonts/clips/likenesses; synthetic-content disclosure; privacy and minors; OAuth scopes/token retention/revocation; API quota and retries; spam/deceptive metadata/artificial engagement; and human review of generated factual claims.

Do not implement policy evasion, copyright circumvention, fake engagement, scraping private data or credential/session theft. Include current official policy sources in the audit output.
